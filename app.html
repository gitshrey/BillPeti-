Legal, Terms & Data Control

1. Terms & Conditions & Limitation of Liability

Bill Store is provided on an "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE" basis as a 100% offline digital bill organizer. Because all bill photos, notes, and party payment balances are stored exclusively on your local mobile device, you are solely responsible for exporting regular backups using the built-in Backup Excel feature. The developer and publisher of Bill Store shall not be liable for any direct, indirect, or incidental damages, loss of business records, device theft/damage, accidental app uninstallation, or accounting discrepancies.

2. Privacy Policy, GDPR & India DPDP Compliance

Zero Cloud Collection: Bill Store does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, bill images, shop names, or financial records on any external server.

Legal Basis & Consent: All data processing occurs locally on your device solely upon your explicit action (capturing/selecting bill photos or recording party payments).

No Cookies or Marketing Trackers: Bill Store uses zero advertising identifiers, analytics trackers, or marketing cookies, and never sends unsolicited marketing communications.

3. Data Retention, Security & Third-Party Sharing

Data Retention: Your records remain in your device's local storage (IndexedDB / localStorage) until you delete them or clear app data.

Third-Party Disclosure: Bill photos and text summaries are shared with third-party applications (such as WhatsApp) strictly when you manually tap the Share on WhatsApp button via Android's native system chooser.

Device Security: Physical access to your phone is protected by your device's own screen lock (PIN/biometrics).
