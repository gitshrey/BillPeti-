Bill Store — Privacy Policy & Terms

100% Offline Privacy: Bill Store does not collect, upload, or sell any personal information, wholesale bills, photos, or party balances on external servers. All data stays locally on your device.

Terms & Backup Responsibility: Bill Store is provided "as is". Because your records are stored locally for privacy, you are responsible for saving regular backups using the built-in Backup Excel feature.

Data Deletion: You can permanently erase all saved bills and balances anytime from Settings → Delete All Data.

Open-Source Acknowledgements: Built with Ionic Capacitor (MIT), Tailwind CSS (MIT), and Lucide Icons (ISC).
